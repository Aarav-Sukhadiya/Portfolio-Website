# Portfolio Design Ideas

This document tracks creative ideas for enhancing the visual aesthetics of the Portfolio website, specifically focusing on the Projects section and empty space filling.

## Selected Aesthetic
* **Font Choice:** `Space Grotesk` (Tech & Geometric) for section headers.

## Background Iteration Queue
We are going to iterate through the following concepts to fill the background space around the project modals. We will test them one by one to find the perfect fit for the "rigid, technical, and precise" aesthetic.

### 1. Interactive Targeting Crosshairs
* **Concept:** Two faint 1px lines (one horizontal, one vertical) span the entire screen and constantly intersect exactly at the mouse cursor.
* **Vibe:** Active radar or scanning interface; gives the user a sense of precision and control over the viewport.

### 2. Blueprint Node Grid
* **Concept:** A full-screen drafting grid made of incredibly faint, large squares. At random intersections of the grid, tiny 2px dots ("nodes") occasionally blink or pulse.
* **Vibe:** Structured engineering canvas; highly rigid and architectural.

### 3. Abstract 3D Wireframe (Technical/Sci-Fi)
* **Concept:** A slow-rotating, monochromatic 3D geometric wireframe (like a torus, icosahedron, or DNA helix) positioned off-center behind the grid.
* **Vibe:** Data Science and AI; explicitly technical but minimal and non-distracting.

### 4. Data Stream / Binary Rain (Hacker)
* **Concept:** A very slow, low-opacity stream of hex codes or data cascading vertically on the far-left and far-right edges of the screen.
* **Vibe:** Linux/AI developer environment; evokes terminal output and raw data flow.
